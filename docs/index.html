AI Pulse is a fully automated, daily newsletter that scrapes and curates the most important developments in artificial intelligence from dozens of trusted sources — including arXiv research papers, TechCrunch, VentureBeat, IEEE Spectrum, The Verge, Wired, and HackerNews. Every morning at 07:00 UTC a GitHub Actions workflow fetches the latest articles, classifies them into five categories (Research, Agents, Products, Industry, Open Source), ranks them by recency and relevance, and regenerates this page. Multi-Source Scraping

A composite score weighs article freshness (decay over 5 days), source quality, and title substance to surface the most impactful stories first.
